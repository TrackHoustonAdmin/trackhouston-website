## **11-12 Girls 4x800m Relay Team**

The 11-12 girls 4x800m relay team of **Kiarra Wafer-Randle, Camille Napier, Camila Vicens and Jalen Elrod** won gold at the USATF National Junior Olympic.

### **Camila Vicens**

**Camila Vicens** of the 11-12 girls had a great year winning 6 national gold medals and one silver.
- Gold - 1500m Race Walk - USATF National Junior Olympic
- Gold - 4x800m Relay - USATF National Junior Olympic
- Gold - 1500m Race Walk - USATF Youth Outdoor
- Gold - 3000m Run - USATF Youth Outdoor
- Gold - 1500m Race Walk - AAU National Junior Olympic - new record 7:12.16
- Gold - 1500m Race Walk - AAU National Club Championships - new record 7:08.99
- Silver - 3000m Run - AAU National Club Championships

### **Camille Napier**

At the AAU National Club Championships, **Camille Napier** set a new record in the 11 year old 3000 meter run with a time of 10:56.00. She also won silver in the 11 year old 3000m at the AAU National Junior Olympic and was part of the 11-12 girls 4x800m relay team that won gold at the USATF Junior Olympic.

### **Ian Gilmore**

At the AAU National Club Championships, **Ian Gilmore** broke the record in the 12 year old 800m in a time of 2:11.61, won the 400m with a time of 53.89 and won gold as part of the 4x100m relay team. Ian also won silver in the 800m at the USATF National Junior Olympic with his best time of the year of 2:08.17 and won bronze in the 400m.

## **13-14 Boys 4x800m Relay Teams**

The 4x800 A-team of **Deonte Bradford, Lucas Bourgoyne, Nathaniel McRae, Solomon Brent** and the B-team of **Andrew Gandy, Audraleous Henderson, Cameron Gilmore and Darrell Simoneaux** dominated their event by winning gold and silver at the USATF Junior Olympic and at the AAU Club Championships. At the AAU Junior Olympic the teams won gold and bronze. These 2 relay teams are the top ranked 4x800m relay teams in the country. This is the third straight year this division has won this event and looks forward to making it 4 in a row.

### **Lucas Bourgoyne**

**Lucas Bourgoyne** set a new record in the 13 year old 3000m at the AAU National Club Championships. In addition, he won gold in the 1500m and as a member of the 4x800m relay team.

### **Deonte Bradford**

**Deonte Bradford** of the 14 year old division set a new AAU Club meet record in the 800m with a time of 1:59.70. He also won gold as being part of the 4x400 and 4x800 meter relay teams.

## **13-14 Boys 4x400m Relay Team**

The 13-14 boys won gold in the 4x400m relay at the 2014 USATF National Junior Olympic. The team of **Nathaniel McRae, Bryson Jackson, Kendon Flowers and Deonte Bradford** also won gold at the AAU National Club Championships and silver at the AAU National Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Girls 4x800m Relay Team**

The Track Houston 15-16 girls 4x800m relay teams of **Dinah Murphy, Janae Simoneaux, Sydney Gandy and Tyler Rose** won gold at the 2014 USATF National Junior Olympic.

### **Akeyla Mitchell**

**Akeyla Mitchell** won gold in the 17-18 Women 200m dash in a time of 23.85 at the 2014 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **AAU National Age Division Champions - 15-16 Boys Division (84 points)**

The 15-16 boys won their division championship and contributed 84 points to Track Houston's National Club Championship award. The boys won 4 first place awards and 37 total medals. The first place awards included the **4x100m (Shamon Ehiemua, Joshua Oglesby, Dominic Lang, Cameron Ehle), 4x800m (Cameron White, Randall Jubilee, Oscar Deleon, Robert Lewis), 100m (Shamon Ehiemua) and High Jump (Caleb Parker).**

### **Shamon Ehiemua**

**Shamon Ehiemua** won gold in the 15-16 boys 100m and silver in the 200m at the USATF National Junior Olympic. He also won silver as part of the 4x100m and a bronze as part of the 4x400m relay. At the AAU National Club Championships, Shamon won gold in the 15-15 Boys 100m and as part of the 4x100 relay team.

### **Isaiah Lucas**

**Isaiah Lucas** won gold in the 15-16 boys 110 hurdles at the USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Boys 4x800m Relay Team**

The 15-16 boys won the 4x800m relay with the team of **Cameron White, Oscar DeLeon, Randall Jubilee and Robert Lewis** at the USATF National Junior Olympic. This is their second year winning this event. They also won gold in the same event at the 2014 AAU National Club Championships.

## **17-18 Men 4x100m Relay Team**

For the second time in 3 years the 17-18 men won gold in the 4x100m relay. The team of **Tracy Watson, Malcolm White, Bryce Deadmon and Malik D. Wilson (not pictured)** ran a time of 40.65.
