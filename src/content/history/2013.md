## Junior Olympic 2013

# 2013 USATF & AAU National Junior Olympic Results



2013 USATF National Junior Olympic Qualifiers

Track Houston has won 8 or more championships for the fourth consecutive year. This year 7 of those championships were won at the USATF National Junior Olympic in Greensboro, NC. Another national championship was won at the AAU Junior Olympic Games in Ypsilanti, MI. This year Track Houston athletes brought home **109 medals from the USATF Junior Olympic.**

## **9-10 Girls 4x400m Relay Team**

"The 9-10 Girls made their mark not only by winning the 4x400m relay but they also broke the record." The team consisted of Dynasty McClennon, Jalen Elrod, Daja Maxey and Maryah Hampton. Dynasty also won silver in the 200m and 400m. Jalen won bronze in the 400m and Maryah captured 8th place in that event.

## **Adaora Nwokeji**

Adaora Nwokeji won the 9-10 Girls long jump with a distance of 13'-9.50".

## **Arianna Stegall**

Arianna Stegall threw the shot put for a distance of 31 feet, winning gold after earning bronze the previous year.

## **Camila Vicens**

"Camila Vicens increased her national gold medal count to 5 in the 1500m race walk." She set a new national record in the 11 Girls division 1500m race walk with a time of 7:17.80. She earned 5th place in the 3000m race and silver in the 1500m race walk at USATF Junior Olympic.

## **Lucas Bourgoyne**

Lucas Bourgoyne of the 11-12 boys won gold in the 3000m race at USATF Junior Olympic and bronze in the 4x800m team. At AAU Junior Olympic, he won the 3000m and took silver in the 800m and 1500m.

## **13-14 Boys 4x800m Relay Team**

The 13-14 Boys 4x800 relay team of Matthew Abrahams, Deonte Bradford, Randall Jubilee and Tyler Litton won gold at USATF Junior Olympic for the second consecutive year.

## **Samiyah Samuels**

Samiyah Samuels won the 15-16 Girls long jump event at USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Boys 4x800m Relay Team**

The 15-16 Boys 4x800 relay team won gold at USATF Junior Olympic with Kendall Brown, Cameron White, Robert Lewis, and Oscar DeLeon.
