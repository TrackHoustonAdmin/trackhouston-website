## **9-10 Boys - Beaux Hudson**

**Beaux Hudson** of the 9-10 Boys won Gold in the 200m and Silver medal in the 100m at the 2021 AAU National Junior Olympic.

## **9-10 Boys 4x100m Relay Team**

The Track Houston 9-10 Boys 4x100m relay team of **Louis Brown III, Beaux Hudson, Jordan Roper** and **Rowan Paige** won Gold at the 2021 AAU National Junior Olympic.

## **11-12 Girls 4x800 Relay Team**

The 11-12 Girls 4x800m relay team of **Bella Rodriguez, Sasha Payne, Mia Rodriguez** and **Riona Street** for winning Gold at the 2021 AAU National Junior Olympic. Coach Payne also pictured.

## **13-14 Girls - Brianna Rivers**

**Brianna Rivers** came away with 4 medals from the 2021 AAU National Junior Olympic. She won Gold in the 14 year old high jump, Bronze in the 200m hurdles, 4th in the Javelin and 4th in the 100m hurdles.

## **15-16 Girls - Jaela Auguste**

**Jaela Auguste** of the 15-16 girls, won Gold in the high jump at the 2021 AAU National Junior Olympic.
