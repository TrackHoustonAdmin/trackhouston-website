## Athletic Honors 2022

# Athletic Honors 2022 - Track Houston Youth Track Club

Year: 2022 2021 2019 2018 2017 2016 2015 2014 2013 2012 2011 2010 2009

## **2022 Athletic Honors**

---

## **9-10 Boys - Beaux Hudson**

**Beaux Hudson** of the Track Houston 9-10 Boys won 3 gold medals at the 2022 USATF National Junior Olympic. He stood at the top of the podium for the 100m, 200m and as part of the 4x100m relay team

## **9-10 Boys 4x100m Relay Team**

The Track Houston 9-10 Boys 4x100m relay team of **Jeremy Foreman II, Beaux Hudson, Aston Brazier** and **Josiah Young** won Gold at the 2022 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **13-14 Boys - Harrison Lowe**

**Harrison Lowe** of the 13-14 Boys, won Gold in the triple jump at the 2022 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **13-14 Boys 4x100 Relay Team**

The 13-14 Boys 4x100m relay team of **Dilyn Smith, Bryderick Robins, Marrel Davis III** and **Chase Stayman**, won gold at the 2022 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Girls Division**

**Nita Koom-Dadzie** won Gold in the 100m at 2022 USATF Junior Olympic. **Brooke Jackson** won Gold in the 15-16 girls 200M at the 2022 USATF National Junior Olympic. **Sydnee Burr** won Gold in the triple jump at 2022 USATF Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Girls 4x100 Relay Team**

The 15-16 Girls 4x100m relay team of **Nita Koom-Dadzie, Avia Jones, Laila Payne** and **Mackenzie Collins** won Gold at the 2022 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Girls 4x400 Relay Team**

The 15-16 Girls 4x400m relay team of **Sarah Pantophlet, Laila Payne, Avia Jones** and **Mackenzie Collins** won Gold at the 2022 USATF National Junior Olympic

## **15-16 Girls 4x800 Relay Team**

The 15-16 Girls 4x800m relay team of **Grace Kowalski, Sarah Pantophlet, Madison Krueger** and **Illona Gabaut-Diziere** won Gold at the 2022 USATF National Junior Olympic. Also pictured is Coach William Valerie.

## **15-16 Boys 4x100 Relay Team**

The 15-16 Boys 4x100m relay team of **LeAnderay Collins II, Benjamin Haywood, Jaden Jenkins** and **Tobi Hasstrup** won Gold at the 2022 USATF National Junior Olympic.
