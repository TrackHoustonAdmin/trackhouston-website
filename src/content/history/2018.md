## **9-10 Boys 4x400m Relay Team**

The 9-10 boys 4x400m relay team won gold at the 2018 USATF National Junior Olympic. Relay members pictured are **Gavin Williams, Derrick McCullar II, Deisnyk McCollough II, Joshua Vilmael**.

## **11-12 Girls 4x100m Relay Team**

The Track Houston 11-12 girls team of **Aniyah Bennett, Johanna Akoto-Ampaw, Jaela Auguste and Destiny Walker** won the 4x100m relay at the 2018 USATF National Junior Olympic in Greensboro, NC. Also pictured are their coaches Finesia Coleman and Candy McClennon.

## **15-16 Girls 4x400m Relay Team**

Congratulations to the Track Houston 15-16 girls 4x400m relay team of **Dynasty McClennon, Jalen Elrod, Alexandra Webster and Taejha Badal** for winning gold at the 2018 USATF National Junior Olympic in Greensboro, NC.

## **15-16 Boys 4x800m Relay Team**

The Track Houston 15-16 boys team of **Phillip Green, Aaron Cooper, Jose Hernandez-Balsamo and Malik Campbell** won the 4x800m relay at the 2018 USATF National Junior Olympic in Greensboro, NC. Also pictured is their coach Keith Evans.

## **17-18 Women 4x800m Relay Team**

The Track Houston 17-18 Women won the gold medal in the 4x800m relay at the 2018 USATF National Junior Olympic in Greensboro, NC. Gold medal team members are **Madison Savoy, Jessica Rabius, Sydney Allen and Alexa Granderson**.
