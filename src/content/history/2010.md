# 2010 USATF National Junior Olympic Results

## **2010 USATF National Junior Olympic Qualifiers**

The 2010 USATF National Junior Olympic competition held in Sacramento, California featured "over 150 Track Houston athletes accompanied by more than 120 Track Houston coaches, staff members, and parents." The organization earned 146 total medals, including 26 gold. The Youth Boys division achieved a record 26 medals for their division and fielded the nation's top 4×400 meter relay team. Kendall Sheffield won gold in the 100 meter hurdles and came close to the national record in the 200 meter hurdles.

## Athletic Honors 2010

# Athletic Honors 2010 - Track Houston Youth Track Club

Year: 2022 2021 2019 2018 2017 2016 2015 2014 2013 2012 2011 2010 2009

## Sub-Bantam Boys

Bryson Stubblefield, Daniel Huery, Joel Delva, Dylan Dawson

The Sub-Bantam Boys 4×100 meter relay team consisting of Joel Delva, Bryson Stubblefield, Daniel Huery, and Dylan Dawson has the fastest time in the country this year in their division. They not only have the the fastest time this year but they also broke the USATF Region 12 meet record with a time of 58.63 which was previously held by the 2007 Track Houston Sub-Bantam Boys.

## Kendall Sheffield

Kendall Sheffield won gold in the 100 meter hurdles and was less than two-tenths of a second off the National record in winning the 200 meter hurdles. Kendall is also recognized as the top hurdler in the country in the Youth Boys division ranked No. 1 in the 200m hurdles and No. 2 in the 100m hurdles. He won his third gold medal as part of the Youth Boys 4×400 meter relay team.

## Youth Boys

USATF National Junior Olympic - 4x400
Mark Barre', Jordan Tolbert, Kendall Sheffield, Montria Brown Jr.

The Youth Boys had a spectacular meet earning a record 26 medals for their age division and has the nation's top 4×400 meter relay team. The Youth Boys had a second 4x400m relay team to medal 4th and had two 4x800m relay teams to medal in the finals.

## Youth Girls

USATF National Junior Olympic - 4x400
Zarriea Willis, Farrin Davis, Taylor Bennett, Alaysah Johnson

Zarriea Willis, Farrin Davis, Taylor Bennett, and Alaysah Johnson were the only Youth Girls 4×400 meter relay team to run under 3:50 with a winning time of 3:49.69 at the National Junior Onlympic in Sacramento California.

## Intermediate Girls

At the Junior Olympic the Intermediate Girls won gold in the 4×100 meter relay and retained their No. 1 ranking in the country. The team consisted of Bealoved Brown, Bria Carter, Mahasin Ahmad, and Rushell Harvey.

## Intermediate Boys

4 x 800 Relay Team
Nicholas Hooper, Jeffrey Perkins, Nathaniel Jones, Xavier Moss

4 x 400 Relay Team
Jeffrey Perkins, Jacob Onyechi, Xavier Moss, Nicholas Hooper

At the Junior Olympic the Intermediate Boys 4×800 meter relay race came down to the finish, with Track Houston 8:01.06 beating Glenarden 8:01.29 by just two-tenths of a second in the final few meters. The 4×400 meter relay race lead went back and forward between Track Houston and other teams but it was Track Houston that pulled away with 100 meters left to win first place.

## Young Women

In the Young Women division, the 4x800 meter relay team ran away from the field to win gold with a time of 9:13.84 at the Junior Olympic in Sacramento California.

## National Team Champions Youth Boys

A streak of 4 National Team titles have been won by the Youth Boys at the USATF Youth Outdoor Nationals. Their recent national championship was awarded to them in Lisle, Illinois after scoring a total of 95 points, 41 more than the second place team. The Youth Boys won gold in the 4×100, 4×400, and 4×800 meter relays and many other medals in individual events.

## National Team Champions Intermediate Boys

The Intermediate Boys won the National Team title with 69.5 points at the USATF Youth Outdoor Nationals. Their championship win included gold in the 4×400 and 4×800 meter relays and silver in the 4x100 meter relay and 800 meters run.
