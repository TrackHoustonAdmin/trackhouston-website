## **2015 Athletic Honors**

## **Sanjana Perungulam** and **Madison Morgan**

**Sanjana Perungulam** (Gold medal) and **Madison Morgan** (Silver medal) of the Track Houston 9-10 Girls had a dominating performance outpacing the third place finisher by nearly a minute in the 1500m Race Walk.

## **9-10 Girls 4x400m Relay Team**

The Track Houston 9-10 Girls 4x400m relay team of **Kameron Butler, A'Nya Charles, Alexia James and Arie Marshall** won gold in a thrilling come from behind race.

## **11-12 Girls 4x100m Relay Team**

The 11-12 Girls team of **Dynasty McClennon, Daja Maxey, A'Niya Galloway and Taejha Badal** won gold in the 4x100m relay. The team also set a new national record in the 4x100m relay with a time of 49.15 at USATF Region 12 in Abilene Texas.

## **11-12 Girls 4x400m Relay Team**

The team of **Jalen Elrod, Daja Maxey, Dynasty McClennon and Taejha Badal** won gold in the 11-12 Girls 4x400m relay at the USATF Junior Olympic.

## **Camila Vicens**

**Camila Vicens** had a busy summer this year competing in the 13-14 Girls division at 4 national meets. Earning 9 medals, she capped off her summer with 2 national records in the 3000m race walk. One record was set at the AAU Club Championships and the other at the AAU Junior Olympic.

## **13-14 Boys 4x400m Relay Team**

The team of **Genson Hooper-Price, Chase Lane, Kacey Barnett and Ian Gilmore** of the 13-14 Boys won gold in the 4x400m relay. This is back to back annual wins at the USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **13-14 Boys 4x800m Relay Team**

For the fourth year in a row, the 13-14 Boys 4x800m relay team won gold. The winning streak was extended by the team of **Darrius Green, Darrell Simoneaux, Christon Hardemon and Ian Gilmore**.

## **Kynnedy Flannel**

**Kynnedy Flannel** had an outstanding performance in the 15-16 Girls division by winning gold in the 100m, long jump and as 4th leg of the 4x100m relay team. She also won silver in the 200m.

## **G'Auna Edwards**

**G'Auna Edwards** won gold in the 15-16 Girls Heptathlon at the USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Girls 4x100m Relay Team**

The 15-16 Girls team of **Kynnedy Flannel, Javlyn Cameron, Ariele Adams and Rachel Hall** won gold in the 4x100m relay.

## **15-16 Girls 4x800m Relay Team**

The 15-16 Girls 4x800m relay team won 1st place at the 2015 USATF Junior Olympic. The gold medal team members are **Alexa Granderson, Dinah Murphy, Mackenzie Duck and Imani Williams**.

## **17-18 Men 4x800m Relay Team**

The 17-18 Men won gold in the 4x800 meter relay at the 2015 USATF National Junior Olympic. The team of **Cameron White, Kenneth Lange, Oscar De Leon and Myles Marshall** ran a time of 7:50.46.
