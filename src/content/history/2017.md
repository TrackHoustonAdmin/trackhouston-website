## **8U Boys 4x100m Relay Team**

The 8U boys 4x100m relay team won gold at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic with a time of 57.55. Relay members pictured are **Rahsan Hawkins, Joshua Vilmael, Jordan Simmons and Derrick McCullar II**.

## **8U Boys 4x400m Relay Team**

The 8U boys won the 4x400m relay at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic in a thrilling finish. Pictured is **Joshua Vilmael, Jalen Kouaho, Dominick Washington and Derrick McCullar II**.

## **11-12 Girls 4x100m Relay Team**

The 11-12 Girls won gold in the 4x100m relay at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic Championships in Lawrence, Kansas. Team members are **Danielle Johnson, Brooke Coleman, Alexia James and Angel Haynes**.

## **Sophie Atkinson**

**Sophie Atkinson** won the 13-14 girls 1500m and 3000m races at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **13-14 Girls 4x100m Relay Team**

The 13-14 Girls won gold in the 4x100m relay at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic Championships in Lawrence, Kansas. Team members are **Anivea Gilbert, A'niya Galloway, Jada Campos and Taejha Badal**.

## **13-14 Girls 4x400m Relay Team**

**Kayla Meyers, Jada Campos, Taejha Badal and Jalen Elrod** of the Track Houston 13-14 girls, won gold in the 4x400m relay at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **Malik Campbell**

**Malik Campbell** won gold in the 13-14 boys 800m run at the 2017 USATF Junior Olympic. In addition, he was part of the 4x400m team that won gold, 4x800m silver medal team and part of the 4x100m team that earned the bronze medal.

## **15-16 Girls 4x800m Relay Team**

The 15-16 Girls 4x800 relay team of **Rebeca Beaird, Kiarra Wafer-Randle, Lindsey Phillips and Wonders Oguejiofor** won gold at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Boys 4x100m Relay Team**

Included in the gold medal count for the 15-16 boys was the 4x100m relay win. The sprint relay team of **Ian Gilmore, Chidera Agim, Genson Hooper-Price and Ja'Den Johnson** are pictured with their coach, **Daryl Sanford**.

## **15-16 Boys 4x400m Relay Team**

The 15-16 Boys won gold in the 4x400m relay at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic and at the AAU Junior Olympic. The 15-16 boys 4x400m B team earned the bronze medal at the USATF Junior Olympic meet and the eighth place medal at the AAU Junior Olympic.

## **Kynnedy Flannel**

**Kynnedy Flannel** of the 17-18 women won gold in the long jump and 200m at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic in Lawrence, Kansas.

## **17-18 Women 4x800m Relay Team**

The 17-18 Women 4x800 relay won gold at the 2017 USATF National Junior Olympic. Pictured are team members **Madison Savoy, Dinah Murphy, Alexa Granderson and Sydney Allen.**
