## Junior Olympic 2016

# Junior Olympic 2016 - Track Houston Youth Track Club

## **2016 USATF/AAU National Junior Olympics**

**USATF: Track Houston took its talent on the road to the west coast earning 9 National Championships.** The total medal count for Track Houston at the USATF National Junior Olympic in Sacramento, California was 141. Although enduring dry heat over 100 degrees, our athletes performed in traditional fashion.

**AAU:** The Houston area won 40 AAU National Junior Olympic championships which was held in Humble, Texas. **Track Houston won an astonishing 16 of those championships.** In addition, Track Houston set four new national records and was awarded a total of 126 medals. None of the other 903 track clubs participating, came close to this kind of success.

## **Sanjana Perungulam**

Sanjana Perungulam won gold in the 11 year old 1500m race walk at the 2016 AAU National Junior Olympic.

## **Jeyden Coleman**

An awesome job was done by Jeyden Coleman winning Gold in the 11 year old shot put at the 2016 AAU National Junior Olympic.

## **Garrick Griffy and Caleb Schaunaman**

Caleb Schaunaman won gold in the 12 year old boys 1500m race walk at the AAU Junior Olympic. Garrick Griffy took the silver medal in the same age division event.

## **11-12 Boys 4x400m Relay Team**

The 11-12 boys won gold in the 4x400m relay at the 2016 USATF National Junior Olympic. Members of the winning team were athletes Joseph Stephenson, Antonio Hall, Jordan Jackson and Joshua Tarver.

## **13 year old Girls Distance**

In the 13 year old girls division, Sophie Atkinson won gold in the 1500m and 3000m, Heidi Nielson won silver in the 1500m and 3000m, and Kevriana Scott won Bronze in the 1500m and 4th in the 3000m at the AAU Junior Olympic. Sophie also won the silver medal in the 13-14 girls 3000m at the USATF Junior Olympic.

## **Camila Vicens**

Camila Vicens continued her national medal collection by winning the 14 year old girls 3000m race walk at the 2016 AAU National Junior Olympic. In that race she also set the new national record to 15:21.48. Camila also earned the Gold medal in the 13-14 girls 3000m race walk at USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **13-14 Girls 4x800m Relay Team**

The team of Camila Vicens, Kevriana Scott, Sophie Atkinson and Jalen Elrod won gold in the 13-14 girls 4x800 relay at the 2016 USATF National Junior Olympic. Also pictured is their coach, Kenya Artis.

## **Elijah Bean**

Elijah Bean won gold in the 13 year old 400m at the 2016 AAU National Junior Olympic in the winning time of 50.98.

## **Ian Gilmore**

Ian Gilmore won gold in the 14 year old 400m at the 2016 AAU National Junior Olympic in a winning time of 49.65.

## **Kynnedy Flannel**

Kynnedy Flannel won the 15-16 girls 200m in a time of 23.59 at the 2016 AAU National Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Girls 4x100m Relay Team**

The 15-16 girls 4x100m team of Kynnedy Flannel, Kailei Collins, Kennedy Gamble and Rachel Hall set a new record in the prelims to 45.64 at the 2016 AAU National Junior Olympic. The next day in the finals, the girls broke their own record setting it to 45.49.

## **15-16 Girls 4x800m Relay Team**

The 15-16 girls won the 4x800m win at the 2016 AAU National Junior Olympic. The winning team was Tamia Badal, Sydney Allen, Jesica Gordon and Alexa Granderson.

## **Solomon Brent**

Solomon Brent won the 15-16 boys 800m race was part of the 4x800m relay team that won gold at the 2016 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **Andrew Gandy**

Andrew Gandy won Gold in the 15-16 boys 3000m race at the 2016 AAU National Junior Olympic.

## **15-16 Boys 4x400m Relay Team**

The 15-16 boys 4x400m relay team of Genson Hooper-Price, Chidera Agim, Sebastian Cooper and Nathaniel McRae won gold at the 2016 AAU and USATF National Junior Olympics.

## **15-16 Boys 4x800m Relay Team**

The 15-16 Boys set a new AAU National Junior Olympic record in the 4x800m relay. The Gold medal team of Ethan Phillips, Deonte Bradford, Solomon Brent and Sebastian Cooper set the new record to 7:55.91. Their coach Daryl Sanford graciously accepts the 4x800m first place plaque. The team also won gold at the 2016 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **17-18 Girls 4x100m Relay Team**

The relay team of Nia Williams, Essance Sample, Charity Thomas and Sierra Smith won gold in the 17-18 girls 4x100 relay at the 2016 AAU and USATF National Junior Olympics.

## **Joe Williams**

Joe Williams, of the 17-18 boys, won gold in the 200m dash at the 2016 USATF and AAU National Junior Olympics. Also pictured is Coach Darcy Davis.

## **17-18 Boys 4x800m Relay Team**

The Track Houston 17-18 boys 4x800 relay team won a second straight year of gold at the USATF National Junior Olympic. This years team consisted of Kenneth Lange, Cameron White, Jordan Vance and Oscar DeLeon
