## 2008 Junior Olympics Results

"Track Houston athletes made an impressive showing at the Junior Olympics which was held in Omaha, Nebraska," earning 132 medals overall. Notable gold medal performances included Alaysha Johnson in the 400 Meter Run (57.11), Midget Girls 4x400 Relay (3:58.24), Intermediate Boys 4x100 Relay (41.67), Young Men 4x400 Relay (3:12.76), and Matthew Kosecki in Discus Throw (58.95m).
