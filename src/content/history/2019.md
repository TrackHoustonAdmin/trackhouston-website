## Athletic Honors 2019

# Athletic Honors 2019 - Track Houston Youth Track Club

Year: 2022 2021 2019 2018 2017 2016 2015 2014 2013 2012 2011 2010 2009

## **8U Boys 4x100m Relay Team**

The 8U boys 4x100m relay team of **Kobie Stewart, Beaux Hudson, Ian Smith** and **Jaylon Francis** won Gold at the 2019 USATF National Junior Olympic. The Track Houston 8U boys won the 4x100m relay 2 out of 3 years.

## **15-16 Girls 4x100m Relay Team**

The Track Houston 15-16 Girls 4x100m relay team of **Anivea Gilbert, Shaniece Walker, Taejha Badal** and **Dynasty McClennon** won Gold at the 2019 USATF National Junior Olympic. Also pictured is their coach William Valerie.

## **17-18 Women - Grace Kohout**

**Grace Kohout** of the 17-18 Women, won Gold in the 3000m and took the 5th place medal in the 1500m at the 2019 USATF National Junior Olympic.

## **17-18 Men - Clayton Keys**

**Clayton Keys** of the Track Houston 17-18 Men won 3 gold medals at the 2019 USATF National Junior Olympic. Clayton stood at the top of the podium for the Long Jump, 400m Hurdles and as part of the 4x400m relay team.

## **17-18 Men 4x400m Relay Team**

The 17-18 Men 4x400m relay team of **Clayton Keys, Ethan Phillips, Ian Gilmore** and **Sir Hubbard**, won gold at the 2019 USATF National Junior Olympic.
