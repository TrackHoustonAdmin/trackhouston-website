## Junior Olympic 2007

# 2007 Junior Olympics Results



Track Houston 2007 Junior Olympic qualifiers

Over 135 Track Houston athletes competed at the Junior Olympics at Mt. San Jacinto College (MtSAC) in southern California. Track Houston again improved it's national position in quality and quantity…135 medals were earned! Our wonderfully talented Bantam Girls placed three (3) teams, yes, 3 teams in the 4x400 relay finals, proving...at Track Houston we know the 4x4! Our elite Primary Boys team earned 22 medals at the USATF Region XII track meet in Odessa breaking several meet records along the way. Thank you sponsors, parents, coaches, managers and administrators for helping us take California by storm.
