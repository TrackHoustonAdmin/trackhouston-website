With the USATF National Junior Olympic Championships held practically in our backyard at Texas A&M University in College Station, Track Houston delivered one of its biggest hauls ever: **170 medals**, including **31 golds**, 13 silvers and 31 bronzes.

Standouts included Jace Coleman with multiple golds in the 11-12 division, Harrison Lowe with triple jump gold and long jump bronze in 15-16, and Dillon Mitchell with 100m gold and 200m bronze in 15-16. Relay squads from 8U through 17-18 put medals on the board across the 4x100m, 4x400m and 4x800m.

Track Houston qualifies more athletes for the national Junior Olympics than any other club in the country — and 2024 showed why.
