Track Houston closed out the 2026 USATF National Junior Olympic Championships with **171 medals**, a **new national record** and **17 national championship wins** — another statement season for the club.

Elijah Zuniga swept gold in the 17-18 Boys 800m and 1500m and anchored the mixed 4x800m relay to a third title. Maryann Akpobassa set a triple jump record and added silver in the long jump. Thomas Holmes III took the 11-12 Boys 400m crown, Jace Coleman won pentathlon gold with a hurdles silver in 13-14, Brooklyn Allen ran away with the 17-18 Girls 3000m, and Avonte Earl took the 17-18 Boys 200m.

The relays delivered too: the 8U Boys 4x100m and 9-10 Girls 4x400m both won gold, and the 11-12 Boys 4x100m squad's **46.85 national record**, set at Region 12, stood through nationals.

Congratulations to every athlete, coach and family who made the trip. This is what "quantity of quality" looks like.
