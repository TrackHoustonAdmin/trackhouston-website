The 2026 cross country season is officially open for registration.

Practices begin **Monday, August 24** at all three cross country sites:

- **Cypress** — John Paul Landing Park · Mon, Tue, Thu · 6:00–7:30 PM
- **Katy** — Willow Fork Park · Mon, Tue, Thu · 6:00–8:00 PM
- **Sugar Land** — Sugar Land Memorial Park · Mon & Tue · 6:00–7:15 PM

The season opens with the **Track Houston CC Invitational on September 5** at John Paul Landing Park and builds toward the USATF National Junior Olympic Cross Country Championships in Lafayette, Louisiana on December 12.

Athletes must be registered before attending practice. Questions? Contact co-head coaches Kenya Artis or Kenneth Gandy.
